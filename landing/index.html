Start in three steps

The first visit downloads the compiler, about 6 MB. After that it opens from your phone's cache.

  1. Open the appTap Launch the app. It works in Chrome, Brave, Safari or Edge.
  2. Write or pick an exampleType your program, or open the ☰ menu for eight ready-made examples.
  3. Tap RunWhen your program asks for input, type in the console and press Enter.
